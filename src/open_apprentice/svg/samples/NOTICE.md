# Sample SVG assets

`product-cards.svg`, `dashboard-tiles.svg` and `pricing-table.svg` were authored for this project (original work, MIT like the repository). They contain only
rectangles, groups, paths, circles and text drawn by hand; no third-party artwork. The real-SVG lane accepts only assets with clear provenance:
owner-authored or independently verified. Nothing from Vector-Bench's excluded scenic illustrations (or any derivative) is, or may be, bundled here.
