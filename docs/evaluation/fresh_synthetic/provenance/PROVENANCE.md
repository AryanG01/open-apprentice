# Provenance and intended use

Created on 27 October 2026 for Aryan Ganju's Open Apprentice hackathon testing and demonstration, at his explicit request to generate the banner pack.

**Creator/method:** ChatGPT's built-in image-generation tool, prompted by the assistant. Four separate new-image calls, one per final banner. No existing banner, uploaded reference photograph, third-party logo or stock asset was supplied to the generator. Original outputs are copied unchanged into `inputs/`; no post-generation crop, resize, retouch or text replacement was performed. Exact prompts are retained in this folder.

**Brands/products:** LUMIQ, ROVENA and TUNORA are names used for fictional test campaigns. The images are not sourced from or presented as endorsements by real businesses. Name uniqueness has not been checked; do not claim ownership of a real brand or a trademark clearance. All prices and “Demo offer” lines are fictional test copy. There are no product-page URLs or actual purchase links.

**Permission/provenance record:** these files were generated specifically in response to the owner's request for assets to use in this project's testing and demo. There is no outside client or photographer whose permission has been obtained or is being asserted. Do not fabricate a client permission letter, stock license, CC0 designation, exclusivity claim or customer provenance. This is a generation record, not a legal rights opinion.
