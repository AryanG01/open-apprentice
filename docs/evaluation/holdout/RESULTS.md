# Held-out result (29 Oct 2026): the frozen code on 17 images nobody tuned on

Protocol: `PROTOCOL.md` (frozen at commit `64df984` before any image was opened). Images: 17 unique JPEGs (WhatsApp-compressed), generated by the owner with ChatGPT from the prompts in
`docs/plan/holdout_prompts.md`; some cases arrived as 2 to 4 variants, so the "first output only" rule was not kept, and **no variant was dropped**. Case H7 was not delivered.
Expected text was annotated by the assistant by eye; built cards were reviewed by a fresh-context reviewer (not the author) against the written checklist. Live models, scripted operator
(edits text that differs from the expected text; never edits a box; split if both layouts fit, else shrink). Cost of the whole run: about $0.06.

## What this means
- Reading is strong and honest refusal worked. Building is not reliable yet: two thirds of ordinary flat-background banners did not reach a card on the first pass, mostly because of how small
  and widely spaced wordmarks are boxed. Against the written target (clean cards on at least 5 of 5 in-envelope cases), **this fails**; the plan's 6-of-8 style Design gate is not met.
- These 17 images are now spent. Everything below is a development change made after seeing them and must not be read as held-out performance.
