# Policy v2 for the apprentice (Plan 7)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (the owner chose native execution). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Test whether giving A (and D, A's policy with uniform questions) more of its unused attempt budget fixes the
dominant failure found in the funded run: every proposal rejected, with only 2.5 of 8 attempts used. A v2 is published as
new systems `A2` and `D2`; the original `A` and `D` rows stay as they are (spec 7: a post-freeze change gets a new version
identifier and the original rows stay published).

**Architecture:** two new `StepConfig` fields, both default off so A is bit-for-bit unchanged:
`max_revisions` (the spec's "one contradiction revision" allowance becomes N) and `tolerate_truncation` (a reply cut at the
completion cap becomes an empty round, not a terminal error). New learner systems `A2` and `D2` set them. No prompt changes
(the proposal prompt is shared with B; B is not re-run).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-teach-correct-reuse-design.md` §4 ("Allowances per cycle: one initial proposal
round, at most one contrastive round, at most one contradiction revision") and §7. **v2 deviates from §4 on purpose**, and
records it in `docs/decisions.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Honesty:** v2 was designed after seeing the final rows, so its final-split numbers are post-hoc and will be labelled
  so everywhere ("A2: a post-freeze policy change"). Development-split numbers are the only pre-registered check.
- **Same budgets:** spec 3.7 unchanged: ≤ 8 provider attempts in a learning cycle (v2 spends more of them, never more).
- **Boundary and rules** as in Plans 5 and 6 (learner never imports the evaluator; no peeking at `benchmarks/final/`).
- **Money:** nothing paid before the owner confirms the balance. Stage 1 (dev check) cap $4.50 real; stage 2 (final re-run of
  A2 and D2) only if the pre-registered criterion below is met and the owner has topped up. The ledger now prices output at
  $3.4/M (the ledger ran 13% low).

## Pre-registered decision rule (fixed before any v2 result)

Stage 1 runs A2 once on 14 dev episodes (catalogue 4, beyond 4, sequential 6). **Go to stage 2 only if both hold:**
1. learning success on the 8 catalogue + beyond episodes is **at least 6/8** (A scored 46% on the final learning episodes);
2. at most **1 wrong commit** in the 14 runs.

Otherwise stop: report v2 as tried and not adopted. Either way the dev rows are committed.

## Review Focus

1. **A is unchanged:** with default config the step policy and executor produce the same actions as before (the existing
   correction and step trace tests, plus a new equality test on scripted logs).
2. **Attempt budget is never exceeded** by extra revision rounds (8 attempts, retries counted).
3. **A truncated reply is recorded honestly** (as a failed round with its cost), never as a success and never dropped from
   the ledger or the diagnostics.
4. **A2 and D2 differ from A and D only in those two settings.**
