words, price, link and picture survived. That is a statement about what the pages say, not a claim that no such feature exists
inside the products, and **no side-by-side run of the same banner through Canva or Adobe has been done yet** (owner action).
Resizing and layer extraction are not novel; the wedge is the verifiable record and asking before choosing.

## Why it matters (sources)
- Vertical 9:16 at 1080 x 1920 is the common size for Stories and Reels ads (several third-party guides agree; platform ad managers are authoritative).
  Safe-zone figures differ by source (Meta guidance relayed by [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/) asks for about the top 14% and, with a disclaimer, up to the bottom 40% to be kept clear). **This project's safe area is 220 px top and bottom and 72 px at the sides, which is narrower than that guidance: a card is not yet guaranteed ad-safe on every placement.**
- Price accuracy is policed: Google Merchant Center disapproves products when the feed price differs from the landing page ([Mismatched product price](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12159029?hl=en), [policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6098334)). This project's page check is a lighter, advisory cousin: it looks for the banner's price among the amounts on the page and says it is not proof of today's offer.
